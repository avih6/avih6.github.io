1. Overview & Privacy Principles

The "Disabled Permit Check" (בדיקת תו נכה) application (the "App") is developed and maintained by avih6 as a free, ad-supported utility application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

The App operates on a strict privacy-first principle: it does not require user registration, does not create personal user accounts, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal names, phone numbers, or residential addresses.

2. Government Data Source & Legal Disclaimer

Independent Search Aid: Disabled Permit Check is an independent utility developed solely by avih6.

• No Government Affiliation: The App is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or operated on behalf of the Israel Ministry of Transport and Road Safety (משרד התחבורה והבטיחות בדרכים), Israel Police, or any governmental or municipal authority.

• Open Data Source: Disabled parking permit verification data is queried in real time from the official Israeli Government Open Data repository (data.gov.il). avih6 does not own, generate, or modify the public registry.

• Informational Use Only: Search results are provided for informational convenience only and do not replace official municipal or police enforcement records or physical permit inspection.

3. Data Handling & Query Processing

3.1 Ephemeral Vehicle Plate Searches

When a vehicle license plate number is entered or scanned:

  • The query is transmitted securely over HTTPS directly to verify records against the public government database (data.gov.il).
  • Searches are processed ephemerally to retrieve matching permit metadata (such as permit status, issue date, or permit type).
  • Search queries are not linked to individual user identities or tracked across sessions.

3.2 Local Storage & Search History

Optional local features (such as recent search history or saved vehicles) are stored exclusively in the device's private local storage (SQLite / SharedPreferences). This data remains on the physical device and is never uploaded to external servers.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

To monitor application stability and support free access, the App utilizes trusted SDKs provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage statistics to monitor feature adoption.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects technical stack traces and OS diagnostic details to diagnose and resolve application crashes.
  • Google AdMob: Serves non-intrusive advertisements. AdMob may process anonymous advertising identifiers (AAID) and coarse IP data in accordance with Google's policies.

Review the Google Privacy Policy for full details on Google's data handling.

6. User Rights & Local Data Management

Because the App does not maintain user accounts or cloud databases, users retain full control over local data:

  • Users may clear saved vehicles and search history at any time from within the App settings.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing application data via Android OS Settings permanently purges all local records from the device.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The App is designed as a utility tool for the general public.

8. Compliance with Israeli Law & International Scope

  • Israeli Privacy Protection Law (חוק הגנת הפרטיות): Processing of data complies with Israeli legal standards. Users residing in Israel possess statutory rights regarding personal data processing.
  • International Scope: The App is designed primarily for use in Israel. To the extent international data protection regulations (such as GDPR) apply, data processing conforms to recognized fair information practices.

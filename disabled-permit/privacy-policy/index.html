1. Overview & Privacy-First Principle

The "Disabled Permit Check" (בדיקת תו נכה) application (the "App") is developed and maintained by avih6 as a free, ad-supported utility application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

The App operates on a strict privacy-first approach: it does not require user registration, does not create cloud user accounts, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or residential addresses.

2. Important Government & Data Source Disclaimer

Independent Search Aid: Disabled Permit Check is an independent utility developed solely by avih6.

• No Government Affiliation: The App is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or operated on behalf of the Israel Ministry of Transport and Road Safety (משרד התחבורה והבטיחות בדרכים), Israel Police, or any governmental or municipal authority.

• Open Data Source: Disabled parking permit verification data is queried in real time from the official Israeli Government Open Data repository (data.gov.il). avih6 does not own, author, host, or modify the public registry.

• Informational Use Only: Search results are provided for informational convenience only. The physical badge displayed on the vehicle windshield, municipal signage, and official enforcement records always take precedence.

3. Data Handling & On-Device Processing

3.1 Ephemeral Vehicle Plate Searches

When a vehicle license plate number is queried:

  • The search request is transmitted securely over HTTPS directly to query the public government database (data.gov.il).
  • Searches are processed ephemerally solely to display matching permit status (such as permit validity and issue date).
  • Search queries are not linked to individual user identities and are not tracked across sessions.

3.2 Camera License Plate Scanning (ML Kit)

The App provides an optional camera feature to scan license plate numbers from vehicle images:

  • On-Device Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Text recognition is performed entirely locally on the physical device hardware using Google ML Kit.
  • No Media Recording: The application does NOT capture, record, store, or transmit camera images or video frames to any external server.

3.3 Local Storage & Watchlist

Optional conveniences (such as recent search history and saved watchlist vehicles) are stored exclusively in the device's private local database (Room SQLite). Background updates (WorkManager) periodically check status changes locally without transmitting user data to external third parties.

6. User Rights & Local Data Management

Because the App does not maintain user accounts or cloud databases, users retain full sovereignty over their local data:

  • Users may clear saved vehicles and search history at any time from within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing application storage via Android OS Settings permanently purges all local records from the device.
  • Users may direct any privacy inquiries to av6development@gmail.com.

2. Nature of Service & Feature Overview

• Mobile Utility Tool: The App is designed to provide illumination features including standard torchlight, strobe pulsing, SOS distress signaling, screen light illumination, morse code transmission, and integrated convenience sensors (Compass and Bubble Level).

3. User Responsibilities & Safety Precautions

SAFETY & PHOTOSENSITIVITY WARNING: Rapid flashing and strobe illumination modes may trigger seizures or discomfort in individuals with photosensitive epilepsy.

• Do not direct high-intensity LED light into human or animal eyes.

• Do not operate strobe features during driving, cycling, or any activity requiring focused visual attention.

• Continuous high-intensity LED light usage naturally increases battery consumption and device warmth. Built-in battery limiters can be adjusted in the App Settings.

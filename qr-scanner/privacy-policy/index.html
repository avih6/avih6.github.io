1. Overview

The "QR Scanner & Generator" application (the "App") is developed and maintained by avih6 as a free utility application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

The App is built on a privacy-first approach: scanning and generating QR codes is performed locally on the physical device without transmitting scan contents or generated codes to external servers.

2. Camera Permission & Scanning

The App requests access to the device camera solely for the purpose of scanning and decoding QR codes and barcodes in real-time:

  • On-Device Processing: The camera video feed is processed entirely locally on the device hardware to detect QR patterns.
  • No Image/Video Recording: The application does NOT record, capture, store, or transmit camera images or video feeds to any external server.

3. Local Data & Offline Processing

Generated QR codes, scan history, and user settings are stored privately and strictly in local device storage. No user content is collected, shared, or sold.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

To monitor application stability and performance, standard industry SDKs provided by Google LLC may process anonymous technical metrics:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Aggregated, anonymous app usage metrics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Technical crash reports and device diagnostics to identify and resolve bugs.
  • Google AdMob: To display non-intrusive advertisements in supported versions, processing advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's policies.

Review the Google Privacy & Terms for more details.

5. Official Distribution Channel

The authorized distribution channel for the App is the Google Play Store. avih6 assumes no responsibility for APK packages obtained from third-party websites or unofficial sources.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

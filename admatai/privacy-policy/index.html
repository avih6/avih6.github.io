1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AdMatai, provided by avih6, handles information when you use the app and its invitation pages. AdMatai lets users calculate service time, manage optional service details, and use social features. You may use the app as an anonymous guest or sign in with Google.

2. Information We Collect

Information you choose to enter can include a display name, service dates, role, rank, beret, unit patch and preferences. Google sign-in may provide a Google account identifier, display name and email address. We do not request a phone number, address, contact list or sensitive military information as a condition of use.

3. App Permissions and Device Access

Notifications are optional and are requested only after an explanation screen. Coarse location is optional and is used on-device for map-related features. Camera access is requested by the system scanner only when you choose to scan an invitation QR code. Selecting a contact only opens the device sharing flow and does not upload your contacts to AdMatai.

4. Service and Community Data

When you create a friendship invitation, Firebase stores a random invitation code, your authenticated account identifier and your display name. The recipient must explicitly accept it. Map reports and lost-and-found posts contain only information you choose to submit. Group statistics are calculated from service fields that members choose to share, such as roles, berets, unit patches and release dates. Do not submit classified, operational, private or third-party personal information.

When you create a group invitation, the group name, your authenticated account identifier and your display name may be stored with the invitation so the recipient can recognize its source. A group may show your first name and selected service fields to its members. Group statistics are calculated only from service fields that members choose to share. Birthday information and other personal details are not shared unless you explicitly choose to add or share them.

To protect the community, AdMatai may remove a Lost and Found post and temporarily or permanently restrict the account's ability to publish in Lost and Found when a user repeatedly or seriously violates the rules, including through harassment, fraud, spam, privacy violations, prohibited content or false reports. This restriction applies only to Lost and Found. It does not block Friends, Groups, the Military Police Detector, IDF-pedia, service calculations or other app features. Access may be restored after review or when the restriction expires.

AdMatai is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Israel Defense Forces or any government body.

5. Third-Party Services

AdMatai uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to provide sign-in, invitations and optional cloud features. It may use Google Play services for Android capabilities. These providers process data under their own policies, including Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Storage and Security

Most service preferences are stored locally on your device. If you sign in and enable cloud backup, selected service data is stored in Firebase for synchronization. We use reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

AdMatai is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account from Settings in the app. This removes the account and associated cloud data that AdMatai controls, subject to limited technical retention needed for security, legal obligations or backups. You can also remove local app data from Android system settings.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The date above indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact av6development@gmail.com.

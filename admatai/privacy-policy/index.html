1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ad Matai!, provided by avih6, handles information when you use the Android app, its invitation pages and its public website. You may use the app as an anonymous guest or sign in with Google.

2. Information Processed by Ad Matai!

Depending on the features you use, Ad Matai! may process a display name, gender, birth date, service dates, role, rank, beret, unit patch, service pins, profile photo and local preferences. Google sign-in may provide a Firebase account identifier, display name, profile photo and email address.

Community features may process friend and group memberships, invitation codes, group names, group roles, Military Police Detector reports with a selected location, details and timestamps, and Lost and Found posts with the information and images you choose to submit. Military Police Detector reports are decoupled from the profile shown to other users and do not expose names or phone numbers in the report UI. Internal account or device identifiers may be retained for report ownership, abuse prevention and statistical processing. Historical and active reports are stored in Firebase to power real-time crowd-sourced radar alerts and calibrate aggregated statistical predictive models (such as hourly risk distributions and hotspot trends). For these models, the service may also process report timing, an approximate distance band, report history and consistency signals to calculate an internal reliability and relevance score. This score is used to weight aggregate insights and is not presented as a public user rating. A Lost and Found contact request can contain a phone number supplied by the sender. That number is stored separately and is readable by the recipient only after the request is accepted.

The website uses Google Analytics to measure page usage and may use browser storage to remember the selected color theme. Google may process technical information and identifiers for these services under its own policies.

3. Permissions and Device Access

Notifications are optional and requested only after an explanation screen. Precise location is used for map features, to show your position on maps and to check that a Military Police Detector report is near the reporting user. The selected report location may be visible to other signed-in users. Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan an invitation QR code. Selecting a contact opens the device sharing flow and does not upload your contact list to Ad Matai!.

4. Community and Group Data

Friend invitations store a random code, the sender's authenticated account identifier and the sender's display name until the invitation is used or otherwise expires. Group invitations store the group name, sender identifier and sender display name so the recipient can recognize the invitation.

Groups may show the display name entered by a member, which may be a full name. Shared role, beret, unit patch, service pins and profile photo follow the member's sharing settings. The current group data model also stores a member's birth date when it is available so the app can calculate the nearest birthday, average age and age range. Do not enter a birth date or profile information into a group if you do not want those details used in group statistics.

Users can block users or content and report users or user-generated content. Shout text is user-generated content and is not automatically filtered phrase by phrase. Do not use it to threaten, harass, expose private information or submit unlawful content. Group owners and authorized admins may manage invitations and, where permitted by the app, member roles. Members may leave a group; current owner restrictions are described in the Terms of Service.

To protect the community, Ad Matai! may remove a Lost and Found post and temporarily or permanently restrict an account's ability to publish in Lost and Found for serious or repeated violations, including harassment, fraud, spam, privacy violations, prohibited content or false reports. This restriction does not block Friends, Groups, the Military Police Detector, IDF-pedia, service calculations or other app features.

Ad Matai! is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Israel Defense Forces or any government body.

5. Third-Party Services

Ad Matai! uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics for sign-in, cloud data, push notifications and crash diagnostics. It may use Google Play services for Android capabilities and OpenStreetMap tiles for maps. The website uses Google Analytics. These providers process information under their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

6. Storage and Security

Many service preferences, rank calculation milestones, smart diagnoser logs, and selected images are stored locally on your device. Signed-in features store selected cloud data in Firebase for synchronization and community functionality. Service profile parameters (including rank timelines, customized milestone dates, service track, and personal notes) synchronized to the cloud are encrypted client-side using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with cloud-backed key tokens to prevent unauthorized access or data loss. In contrast, Military Police Detector reports are public crowd-sourced community broadcasts intended for all users; however, they are strictly anonymized and contain no user identity or profile metadata. Ad Matai! uses reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Website theme preference is stored in browser storage.

7. Children's Privacy

Ad Matai! is not directed to children under 13. Ad Matai! does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Retention and Deletion

You can request account deletion from Settings in the app, including while a Lost and Found publishing restriction is active. Account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and the personal profile document that the app deletes directly. Community records such as group membership, invitations, friendship records, reports, posts or contact requests may be retained, removed or anonymized according to their record-specific rules, safety needs and legal obligations. Account deletion does not guarantee automatic removal of every historical community record.

Aggregated and de-identified community statistics (such as historical Military Police frequency metrics and predictive distribution models) may be retained in anonymized form to maintain predictive accuracy for all users even after an individual user account is deleted. Limited security records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to prevent abuse, handle disputes, comply with law or protect the service. A raw email address is not retained solely as a ban list. You can also remove local app data from Android system settings.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact avih6 to make a request. Account ownership verification may be required. An explanation may be provided when community records cannot be removed immediately for safety, legal or operational reasons.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, website or legal requirements change. The date above indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

2. Data Collection & Usage

No Personal Data Collected

WaveTune Radio does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), including but not limited to:

  • Name, email address, or account credentials
  • Location or GPS data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs (beyond what the Android OS may provide to analytics)
  • Contact lists or call logs
  • Financial information

No account registration or sign-in is required to use WaveTune Radio.

Local Storage & Preferences

The following data is stored exclusively on your device (using Android SharedPreferences/DataStore) and is never transmitted to any server:

  • Your favorite radio stations and genres
  • Recently played stations history
  • App settings (theme, language, equalizer settings, sleep timer preferences)
  • Alarm clock configuration

This data remains on your device unless you uninstall the app, at which point it is deleted.

Analytics

WaveTune Radio may use Firebase Analytics (by Google) to collect anonymized, aggregated usage statistics such as:

  • App open/close events
  • General feature usage (e.g., how often the sleep timer is used)
  • Crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics

3. Network Requests & Radio Data

When you use WaveTune Radio, the app makes network requests to:

  • Fetch the list of radio stations, tags, and countries from the RadioBrowser public API
  • Stream audio from the selected radio station's URL
  • Load station logos/icons from external URLs

These requests are standard internet connections. Your IP address may be visible to these external services as part of normal internet communication, just as with any web browser.

WaveTune Radio does not send any personal data, search queries, or usage patterns to these external services.

4. App Permissions

WaveTune Radio requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET — Required to stream radio stations and fetch station data.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — Required to play audio in the background.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 12 and below) — Required only for saving recorded audio files to your device storage.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Required to show the media player notification (required on Android 13+).
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — Required for the alarm clock feature to survive device restarts.
  • USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — Required for the alarm clock to fire at the correct time.

None of these permissions are used to collect personal data.

5. Stream Recordings

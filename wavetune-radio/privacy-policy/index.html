1. Overview & Privacy-First Principle

The "WaveTune Radio" application (the "App") is developed and maintained by avih6 as a free utility application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

The App operates on a strict privacy-first approach: it does not require user account registration, does not create cloud profiles, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user location coordinates.

2. Important Operational & Broadcast Disclaimer

Independent Audio Player & Directory Browser: WaveTune Radio is an independent mobile application developed solely by avih6.

• Third-Party Broadcast Content: The application functions as an audio tuner and directory browser connecting to public internet streams. avih6 does NOT own, produce, host, broadcast, rebroadcast, or monitor the audio content, music, talk shows, news, opinions, or advertisements transmitted by independent radio stations.

• Broadcaster Intellectual Property: All radio broadcasts, trademarks, and station brand names remain the exclusive intellectual property of their respective owners and broadcasters.

3. Data Handling, Media Playback & Local Storage

3.1 Foreground Service Permission & Media Playback

The App utilizes the AndroidX Media3 / ExoPlayer framework via an Android Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) strictly to enable uninterrupted background audio playback and display lock-screen media controls.

When a station is selected, the application establishes a direct stream connection between the user's device and the third-party broadcaster's streaming server. No audio streams are routed through or stored on developer-owned proxy servers.

3.2 Alarm Permissions & Local Audio Recordings

• On-Device Stream Recording: If the user utilizes the built-in recording feature, audio files are saved strictly to the physical device's local storage for personal, offline listening. Recordings are never uploaded, synchronized, or transmitted to any external server.

• Exact Alarms & Sleep Timer: Scheduled radio alarms and sleep timers are managed strictly by the local Android OS alarm subsystem (USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM).

3.3 Local Database & Preferences

User preferences (including favorited stations, recent search history, audio buffer settings, and theme choices) are stored strictly locally on the device using a Room SQLite database and Jetpack DataStore. This data is never transmitted to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Technical Data Collection

4.1 Technical Identifiers & Analytics

To ensure app stability, monitor performance, and improve user experience, the app incorporates reputable industry-standard SDKs provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Collects aggregated, pseudonymous usage statistics (such as screen views, station category clicks, and playback durations). Firebase automatically derives coarse, city-level geographic data from IP addresses on Google servers without accessing device GPS. This aggregated data is not linked to any personal user identity.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring: Automatically collects crash logs, stack traces, and device hardware/OS specifications to troubleshoot and resolve technical issues. Diagnostics and crash reports are retained and expired in accordance with standard Firebase retention policies.

4.2 Radio Directory & Content Delivery Networks

To retrieve station listings, category directories, and visual metadata, the App communicates with external public services:

  • Community Radio Browser API: Queries public station directories (radio-browser.info) ephemerally over HTTPS to fetch station stream URLs and names.
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): Station logos, favicons, and country flag graphics are fetched from public CDN endpoints.

6. User Rights & Local Data Management

Because the App operates without user accounts and stores no personal data on external servers, data control is managed directly on the local device:

  • Local Data Removal: Clearing application storage via Android OS Settings or uninstalling the App immediately and permanently erases all locally stored records, history, recordings, and preferences from the physical device.
  • Anonymous Diagnostics: Technical telemetry and crash reports processed by Firebase are pseudonymous, aggregated, and automatically expired according to Google's retention schedules; they cannot be linked to individual user identities or email addresses.
  • Privacy Inquiries: Users may direct any privacy inquiries to av6development@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

Security of user information is prioritized, and commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are employed. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% immune to vulnerabilities, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children’s Privacy

Features

🌍

Worldwide Stations

Discover and stream radio stations from over 150 countries, with real-time data from the RadioBrowser community database.

🎵

Browse by Genre

Filter by Music, News, Talk, Jazz, Rock, Classical, Hip Hop, Latin, and many more genres.

🗺️

Browse by Country

Explore stations grouped by country, including cities and regions — perfect for finding local content abroad.

❤️

Favorites & History

Save your favorite stations and genres for quick access. Your recently played stations are always just a tap away.

⏱️

Sleep Timer & Alarm

Fall asleep to your favorite station with the built-in sleep timer. Wake up to live radio with the radio alarm clock.

⏺️

Stream Recording

Record any live stream directly to your device, saved as high-quality audio files you can play back anytime.

🎛️

Equalizer

Fine-tune your audio experience with the built-in equalizer and volume normalization.

📡

Background Playback

WaveTune Radio plays in the background with full media controls in the notification shade and lock screen.

2. Description of Service

WaveTune Radio is a free Android application that allows users to:

  • Discover and stream live radio stations from around the world via the public RadioBrowser community database
  • Browse stations by country, genre, language, and other filters
  • Save favorite stations for quick access
  • Record live radio streams to their device
  • Use a sleep timer and alarm clock with radio station playback

The App is provided free of charge. No user account or registration is required.

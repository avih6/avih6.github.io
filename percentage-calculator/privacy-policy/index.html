1. Overview & Privacy-First Principle

The "Percentage Calculator" application (the "App") is developed and maintained by avih6 as a free mathematical utility application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

The App operates on a strict privacy-first approach: it does not require user account registration, does not maintain cloud user databases, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations.

2. On-Device Mathematical Processing & Local Storage

2.1 Local Calculation Processing

All numeric inputs, formulas, percentage changes, discount rates, margin calculations, and tip splits are computed entirely on the local device hardware. No calculation entries, numeric values, or mathematical results are ever transmitted to external servers.

2.2 Calculation History & Preferences

The App includes optional calculation history tracking and user customization options (such as decimal rounding precision, number formatting, and UI theme options). All history entries and preferences are stored strictly in private local storage on the device. Users can clear their calculation history at any time using the in-app clear feature or through Android OS Settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Technical Platform Data

The App is distributed through the Google Play Store and relies on standard Google Play Services for distribution and basic platform diagnostics. Google may process non-identifying technical information (such as Android OS version and device model) to maintain platform compatibility.

5. User Rights & Local Data Management

Because the App operates without user accounts and stores no personal data on external servers, data control is managed directly on the local device:

  • Local Data Removal: Clearing application storage via Android OS Settings or uninstalling the App immediately and permanently erases all locally stored calculation logs, history, and preferences from the physical device.
  • Anonymous Diagnostics: Technical telemetry processed at the operating system level is pseudonymous, aggregated, and automatically expired according to standard platform retention schedules; it cannot be linked to individual user identities or email addresses.
  • Privacy Inquiries: Users may direct any privacy inquiries to av6development@gmail.com.

6. Data Security

Security of user information is prioritized, and commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are employed. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% immune to vulnerabilities, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Service is designed for a general audience. There is no intentional collection or solicitation of personal information from children under the age of 13. If it is discovered that a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental consent, please send a notification immediately to the contact email so that the information can be promptly removed.

8. International Privacy Regulations & Scope

  • European & UK GDPR Compliance: The Application complies with European GDPR and UK GDPR principles through strict data minimization, an account-free architecture, and zero persistent user tracking.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): The App does not sell or share personal consumer information. Users retain full rights to query data handling policies under applicable US state privacy frameworks.

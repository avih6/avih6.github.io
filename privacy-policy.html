1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the official website hosted at https://avih6.github.io and all Android applications developed and published by avih6 on Google Play (collectively, the "Services").

Privacy is prioritized across all Services. Core application data and user configurations are handled locally on your device whenever possible.

2. Website Visitor Analytics

This portfolio website uses Google Analytics (ID: G-650BH6MCPS) to aggregate anonymous website traffic data (such as page views, browser type, and country of origin). This information is used solely to evaluate website performance and user engagement. No personally identifiable information is collected or tracked on this website.

3. Mobile Applications Privacy

Each application published by avih6 is designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • Local Storage: Core data (such as calculation history, saved logs, and app settings) is stored locally on your device in a private database.
  • Optional Cloud Backup: For applications that offer optional Google Sign-In and Cloud Backup (e.g., New Driver), your data is encrypted and backed up securely to Firebase Cloud Firestore. Access is strictly limited to your own authenticated account.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Applications may integrate standard third-party SDKs for technical stability, analytics, and advertising:

  • Google AdMob: Used to serve advertisements. AdMob may process device identifiers and IP addresses. In applicable regions (EEA/UK), ad personalization strictly respects user consent provided via GDPR consent dialogs.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Used to process anonymous crash reports and performance metrics to resolve technical bugs.

For more details, please review Google’s Privacy & Terms.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Services do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Applications intended for general audiences or families comply with applicable Google Play Families Policy guidelines.

1. Introduction

Welcome to My List (referred to as "the App"), developed by avih6 ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

My List is a smart utility application created to help you plan, organize, and manage grocery and shopping lists, either independently or collaboratively with family and roommates.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Data: You can use the App anonymously as a guest without providing any personal data such as your name, email address, or phone number. If you choose to sign in with your Google Account, we receive your public display name, email address, and account identifier solely to enable cross-device synchronization and group identification.

Shopping Content: Item names, quantities, units, optional item notes, category labels, and completion checkmarks entered by you are saved to manage your shopping list.

Technical Data: Anonymized crash logs and basic performance diagnostics may be collected automatically by Google Play Services and Firebase to ensure application stability.

3. Authentication & Account Options

The App offers two flexible authentication methods powered by Google Firebase Authentication:

  • Anonymous Guest Mode: Generates a random session identifier without collecting your email or personal identity, allowing you to create lists and join groups while preserving privacy.
  • Google Sign-In: Securely authenticates you with your Google account. Your account credentials and passwords are never accessible to the App.

4. Cloud Synchronization & List Sharing

My List allows users to create shared lists and collaborate in real-time:

  • Each shared list is identified by a unique 6-character alphanumeric invite code.
  • Anyone with whom you share the invite code can view, add, check off, and remove items from that shared list.
  • Access to shared data is enforced via Google Cloud Firestore Security Rules, ensuring that only authenticated users belonging to the group can read or write items.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses trusted third-party services that may process technical data:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform APIs and updates. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase: Provides secure authentication, Cloud Firestore real-time database, and stability diagnostics. (Firebase Privacy Details)

6. Data Storage & Security

All shopping list data is cached locally on your device using Android Room SQLite for fast offline access. Synchronized data is transmitted using encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections and stored on secure Google Cloud Platform infrastructure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. Items can be checked off or deleted at any time. You can leave any shared group whenever you choose. To request complete deletion of any cloud-associated account data, please contact us at the email below.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of your data. You can exercise these rights directly within the App or by contacting us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

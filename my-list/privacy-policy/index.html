1. Overview & Privacy-First Principle

The "My List" application (the "App") is developed and maintained by avih6 as a utility application. This Privacy Policy outlines the standards and procedures governing data handling and user privacy when using the App.

The App operates on a strict privacy-first principle: full functionality is accessible without mandatory registration, personal profiles, or unnecessary data collection.

2. Data Handling & Information Processed

Account Credentials: Users may use the App anonymously in guest mode without submitting personal identity details such as full names, email addresses, or phone numbers. When Google Sign-In is selected, basic authentication identifiers (public display name, email address, and authentication token) are processed solely to facilitate multi-device synchronization and group identification.

Shopping List Content: Grocery item names, quantities, measurement units, optional notes, categories, and completion states are stored to enable list management and real-time collaboration.

Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized diagnostic logs and crash statistics may be collected automatically via Google Play Services and Firebase to maintain stability.

3. Authentication & Account Options

The App offers two flexible authentication methods powered by Google Firebase Authentication:

  • Anonymous Guest Mode: Generates a random session identifier without collecting your email or personal identity, allowing you to create lists and join groups while preserving privacy.
  • Google Sign-In: Securely authenticates you with your Google account. Your account credentials and passwords are never accessible to the App.

4. Cloud Synchronization & List Sharing

My List allows users to create shared lists and collaborate in real-time:

  • Each shared list is identified by a unique 6-character alphanumeric invite code.
  • Anyone with whom you share the invite code can view, add, check off, and remove items from that shared list.
  • Access to shared data is enforced via Google Cloud Firestore Security Rules, ensuring that only authenticated users belonging to the group can read or write items.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses trusted third-party services that may process technical data:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform APIs and updates. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase: Provides secure authentication, Cloud Firestore real-time database, and stability diagnostics. (Firebase Privacy Details)

6. Data Storage & Security

All shopping list data is cached locally on your device using Android Room SQLite for fast offline access. Synchronized data is transmitted using encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections and stored on secure Google Cloud Platform infrastructure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. Personal information from children is not knowingly collected or solicited.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Users maintain complete control over list data. Items can be modified or permanently removed at any time. Users may leave shared groups at will. Inquiries regarding data removal may be directed to the contact email provided below.

9. User Rights

Pursuant to applicable data protection standards (including GDPR and CCPA), users retain rights to access, update, or request the deletion of associated data. Inquiries may be submitted via the contact details below.

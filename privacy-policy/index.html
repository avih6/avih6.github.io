1. Overview & Privacy-First Principle

This Privacy Policy informs visitors regarding the privacy principles and data practices of the avih6 Website (hosted at https://avih6.github.io), developed and maintained by avih6 as a free informational platform to showcase mobile applications. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

The website operates on a strict privacy-first approach: it does not require user account registration, does not maintain user tracking databases, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or private user credentials.

2. Website Operations & Local Data Handling

2.1 Client-Side Browser Execution

The website consists of static client-side web technologies (HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript) executed entirely in the visitor's local web browser. No server-side forms or data harvesting scripts are deployed on this website.

2.2 Local Storage & Theme Preferences

The visitor's display preference (dark mode or light mode) is stored strictly locally in the web browser's localStorage. This data remains on the physical device and is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Telemetry & Web Analytics

3.1 Google Analytics (GA4) & Anonymized Metrics

To monitor website availability and evaluate aggregated usage trends, the website integrates Google Analytics (GA4):

  • Aggregated Statistics: Google Analytics processes pseudonymous technical indicators (such as page views, visitor country/city region, referring URLs, and browser types) to maintain and optimize website performance.
  • No Personal Linkage: Analytics telemetry is pseudonymous and is not linked to personal user identities. Google automatically anonymizes log data by removing portions of IP addresses after 9 months and cookie data after 18 months.

3.2 Third-Party Privacy Policy References

For more information on the data processing practices of Google platform services, review the official policy:

  • Google Privacy & Terms

4. Official Hosting & External Application Links

The website is hosted on secure, distributed infrastructure provided by GitHub Pages (https://avih6.github.io) and provides links to official Google Play Store listings.

Dedicated Mobile Application Policies: Every mobile application featured on this website maintains its own dedicated, specialized Privacy Policy tailored to its specific hardware permissions, local database architecture, and integrated SDKs. Visitors are encouraged to review the respective policy on each application's dedicated sub-page.

5. User Rights & Local Data Management

Because the website operates without user accounts and stores no personal data on external servers, data control is managed directly on the local browser:

  • Local Data Removal: Clearing browser cache, cookies, and local site data via web browser settings immediately erases stored theme preferences.
  • No Server-Side Records: The website does not collect, transmit, or maintain any personal data or usage logs on external servers; therefore, no server-side records exist to access, modify, or delete.
  • Privacy Inquiries: Visitors may direct any privacy inquiries or questions regarding website applications to av6development@gmail.com.

6. Data Security

Security of user information is prioritized, and commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are employed. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% immune to vulnerabilities, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. The website is served exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections via GitHub Pages infrastructure.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Service is designed for a general audience. There is no intentional collection or solicitation of personal information from children under the age of 13. If it is discovered that a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental consent, please send a notification immediately to the contact email so that the information can be promptly removed.

8. International Privacy Regulations & Scope

  • European & UK GDPR Compliance: The website respects the rights of visitors located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) through strict data minimization and zero persistent personal data tracking.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): The website does not sell or share personal consumer information. Visitors retain full rights to query data handling policies under applicable US state privacy frameworks.

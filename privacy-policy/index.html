1. Overview & Developer Portfolio

This Privacy Policy informs visitors regarding the privacy principles and data practices of the avih6 Developer Portfolio (hosted at https://avih6.github.io).

The website is maintained strictly as an informational portfolio to showcase mobile applications developed and maintained by avih6. No user registration is required, no user accounts exist, and no personal data is collected or harvested on this website.

2. Visitor Analytics & Pseudonymous Metrics

To monitor portfolio availability and understand high-level traffic trends, the website integrates Google Analytics (GA4):

  • Aggregated Data: Google Analytics processes non-identifying, aggregated metrics such as page views, visitor country/city region, referring URLs, and browser types.
  • No Personal Profiling: This telemetry is not linked to any personal identity, name, or email address.
  • Documentation: For more details on Google's data processing, review Google Privacy & Terms.

3. External Links & Dedicated Application Policies

This website provides direct hyperlinks to application landing pages and official Google Play Store listings.

Every mobile application maintains its own dedicated, specialized Privacy Policy tailored to its specific hardware permissions, local database architecture, and integrated SDKs. Visitors are encouraged to review the respective policy on each application's dedicated sub-page.

4. User Rights & Data Control

Because the website operates without user accounts and maintains no server-side personal profiles, there are no private user records to access, modify, or delete. Visitors may direct any privacy inquiries or questions regarding portfolio applications to av6development@gmail.com.

5. Data Security

Security of website visitors is prioritized. The website is hosted on secure, distributed infrastructure provided by GitHub Pages and is served exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections to safeguard communications against interception.

6. Children’s Privacy

The portfolio is designed for a general audience. There is no intentional collection or solicitation of personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Policy Changes & Transfer of Ownership

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically by posting the revised version on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. In the event of a transfer of ownership or portfolio reorganization, information practices will remain governed by this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview & Privacy-First Principle

The "Hofesh!" (חופש!) application (the "App") is developed and maintained by avih6 as a free, completely ad-free utility application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

The App operates on a strict privacy-first approach: it does not require user registration, does not create cloud user accounts, contains no advertisements, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user locations.

2. Important Government & Education Disclaimer

Independent Schedule Aid: Hofesh! is an independent utility tool developed solely by avih6.

• No Government Affiliation: The App is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or operated on behalf of the Israel Ministry of Education (משרד החינוך), the Government of Israel, or any municipal educational department.

• Informational Schedule Aid: Holiday schedules, school vacation dates, and countdown timers are provided for personal educational and organizational convenience only.

• Precedence of Official Circulars: Official circulars, schedules, and publications issued directly by the Israel Ministry of Education or relevant school administrations always take precedence over application countdowns.

3. Data Handling & Local Storage

3.1 Local Preferences & Custom Countdowns

When using the App, user preferences and custom targets are processed strictly on the physical device:

  • Local Targets: Custom holiday countdown targets, display preferences, and local reminder settings are stored exclusively in private local storage on the device.
  • No Cloud Accounts: Personal target dates and preferences are never transmitted to, synchronized with, or stored on external servers.

3.2 Ephemeral Jewish Calendar Synchronization (Hebcal)

The App queries public holiday and Jewish calendar schedules in real time over secure HTTPS from the public Hebcal API (api.hebcal.com). These requests are processed ephemerally solely to display accurate holiday dates. No personal identifiers, user profiles, or device identifiers are transmitted or tracked during these queries.

4. Third-Party Services & Technical Data Collection

4.1 Technical Identifiers & Analytics

To monitor application stability, maintain performance, and resolve technical defects, the App incorporates reputable SDKs provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage statistics (such as screen views and feature interaction counts).
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Performance Monitoring: Collects technical stack traces, error reports, and hardware diagnostic details to diagnose and resolve application crashes.

4.2 External Calendar Services

  • Hebcal API: Used strictly for real-time ephemeral retrieval of public Jewish holiday schedules and Hebrew dates.
  • No Advertising SDKs: The App contains no advertising networks, no AdMob SDK, and processes no advertising identifiers (AAID).

6. User Rights & Local Data Management

Because the App does not maintain user accounts or cloud databases, users retain full sovereignty over their local data:

  • Uninstalling the App or clearing application storage via Android OS Settings permanently purges all local records and preferences from the device.
  • Users may direct any privacy inquiries or deletion requests to av6development@gmail.com. If no confirmation is received, please send a reminder email with "Data Deletion Reminder" in the subject line.

7. Data Security

Commercially reasonable administrative and technical measures (including secure HTTPS communication) are implemented to protect information. However, no electronic storage or internet transmission is completely infallible.

8. Age-Appropriate Design & Children’s Privacy

The App is designed as a utility tool for a general audience across various age groups, including students, parents, and families. Because the App collects no personal information, creates no user profiles, and contains no advertisements, no personal data is collected or processed from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes personal data was inadvertently submitted, contact av6development@gmail.com immediately so that the information can be promptly investigated and removed.

9. Compliance with Israeli Law & International Scope

  • Israeli Privacy Protection Law (חוק הגנת הפרטיות): Processing of data complies with the provisions of Israeli law (חוק הגנת הפרטיות, התשמ"א-1981).
  • European GDPR / UK GDPR Scope: The Service is intended primarily for audiences in Israel and is not actively directed to the European Union. To the extent that personal data of individuals located in the European Economic Area (EEA) is processed under circumstances where the GDPR applies, such processing shall comply with the GDPR.

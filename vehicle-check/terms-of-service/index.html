2. Nature of Service & Feature Overview

• Vehicle Information Utility: Vehicle Check is a mobile utility designed to assist users in querying public motor vehicle records in Israel by entering or scanning a vehicle license plate number.

• Features: The App provides access to vehicle technical specifications, previous inspection mileage, road test validity dates, active safety ratings, open recall notices, and an offline OBD-II diagnostic fault code (DTC) lookup tool.

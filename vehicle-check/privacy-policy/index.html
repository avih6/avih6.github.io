1. Overview & Privacy-First Principle

The "Vehicle Check (בדיקת רכב)" application (the "App") is developed and maintained by avih6 as a free, ad-supported vehicular information utility. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "AS IS".

The App operates on a strict privacy-first approach: it does not require user account registration, does not maintain remote user databases, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user GPS location data.

2. Important Legal & Government Disclaimer

NON-GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY DISCLAIMER: Vehicle Check (בדיקת רכב) is an independent utility developed and maintained privately by avih6. The Application is NOT an official government application, does NOT represent, and is NOT affiliated with the State of Israel, the Ministry of Transport and Road Safety (משרד התחבורה והבטיחות בדרכים), the Israel Police, or any governmental agency.

• Public Open Data Source: All vehicular specifications, MOT test validity dates, technical parameters, safety scores, mileage records from previous inspections, and recall statuses presented in the App are retrieved directly from the official Israeli Open Government Data portal (data.gov.il), published under the Government Open Data Framework.

• Direct Public Source Reference: Official government databases may also be accessed directly via data.gov.il - Private and Commercial Vehicles Registry.

3. Data Handling, Hardware Permissions & Local Storage

3.1 License Plate Queries & Open Data Retrieval

When a user searches for a vehicle by license plate number, the App transmits a secure HTTPS query directly to the public API endpoints of data.gov.il. The query is processed ephemerally to retrieve publicly available technical specifications. avih6 does not store, log, or harvest searched license plate numbers on private external servers.

3.2 Camera Permission & Real-Time On-Device Plate Scanning

• Camera Hardware Access: The App offers an optional camera-based optical character recognition (OCR) feature to allow scanning vehicle license plates without manual typing.

• Zero Image Storage & Streaming: Optical text recognition is processed 100% locally on the device using Google ML Kit on-device machine vision. The App never records, stores, photographs, streams, or transmits camera images or video feeds to external servers.

3.3 Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC) Offline Database

The vehicle fault code dictionary (OBD-II / DTC) is stored completely offline inside the local application assets. Searching fault codes is executed 100% on the device hardware without sending diagnostic queries over the internet.

3.4 Wikimedia Vehicle Showcase

To provide visual context for vehicle models, the App fetches publicly licensed model preview graphics via secure HTTPS connections from the Wikimedia Commons API. No user data is transmitted during image retrieval.

3.5 Local Search History & Preferences

Searched vehicle records and customization preferences (such as dark/light themes) are stored strictly locally in private application storage on the physical device (Room SQLite database). Users can clear their search history at any time using the in-app clear action.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Technical Data Collection

4.1 Google AdMob & Android Privacy Sandbox

To support the application as a free utility, the App integrates Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive advertisements:

  • Google AdMob: AdMob may process advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AAID) and coarse IP data to serve and measure advertisements in compliance with its privacy policies. Google automatically anonymizes log data by removing portions of IP addresses after 9 months and cookie data after 18 months.
  • Privacy Sandbox for Android: The application supports modern privacy-preserving advertising APIs in Android 13+ (such as AdServices Topics and Attribution APIs) designed to limit cross-app tracking.

6. User Rights, Data Control & Deletion

Because the App operates without user accounts and stores no personal data on external servers, data control is managed directly on the local device:

  • Local Data Removal: Users can permanently delete their local vehicle search history directly within the app using the "נקה היסטוריה" (Clear History) button or by clearing application storage in Android OS Settings.
  • No Server-Side Records: The App does not collect, transmit, or maintain any personal profiles or usage records on external servers; therefore, no server-side records exist to access, modify, or delete.
  • Privacy Inquiries: Users may direct any privacy inquiries to av6development@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

Security of user information is prioritized, and commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are employed. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% immune to vulnerabilities, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Service is designed for vehicle owners, prospective buyers, and drivers (typically ages 16 and older). There is no intentional collection or solicitation of personal information from children under the age of 13. If it is discovered that a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental consent, please send a notification immediately to the contact email so that the information can be promptly removed.
